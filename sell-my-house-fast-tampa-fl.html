Tampa specifics

  • Foreclosure sales. Florida foreclosures go through the court. In Hillsborough County, the Clerk of Court runs sales online at hillsborough.realforeclose.com every weekday at 10:00 a.m. A sale is canceled if the lender's side doesn't pay the clerk's sale fee by 8:00 a.m. that morning, which is one reason dates can shift.
  • Probate. Estates are heard in the circuit court of Florida's Thirteenth Judicial Circuit, in its Probate, Guardianship, Mental Health and Trust Division. The Hillsborough County Clerk keeps the files, with in-person service in downtown Tampa, Brandon and Plant City.
  • Property taxes. The Hillsborough County Tax Collector (hillstaxfl.gov) collects real estate taxes. Taxes are due November 1 and delinquent if unpaid by April 1. The office then holds a tax certificate sale, and the tax deed process can begin two years after the taxes went delinquent.
  • Code enforcement. The City of Tampa's Neighborhood Enhancement Division, formerly the Code Enforcement and Clean City divisions, enforces the city's non-criminal ordinances, codes and regulations.

If foreclosure is part of your story, talk with your lender and a HUD-approved housing counselor about every option. A sale is one choice, and we never ask for money up front.

Questions sellers ask

How do I sell my house fast in Tampa?
Selling directly to a cash buyer is usually the quickest route because there’s no listing period or mortgage approval. We make a written offer, usually within 24 hours, and can close in as little as two weeks when the title is clear.
When are Hillsborough County foreclosure sales held?
The Hillsborough County Clerk holds foreclosure sales online every weekday at 10:00 a.m. at hillsborough.realforeclose.com. Each sale date is set in the court’s final judgment, and if you have one, send it to us and we’ll tell you honestly what’s possible.
Can I sell a house with a tax certificate on it in Florida?
Often, yes. Delinquent taxes, including the amount tied to a tax certificate, are typically paid from the sale proceeds at closing. Tell us what’s owed and we’ll build it into our offer.
Do cash home buyers in Tampa charge fees?
Some do, so read the contract. We don’t charge fees or commissions, we pay typical closing costs, and you never pay us anything up front.
Will you buy a Tampa house with storm or water damage?
Yes. We buy houses as-is, including ones with roof, flood or water damage. We see the house first, so its condition is already reflected in the offer.
