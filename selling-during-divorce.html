Selling the House During a Divorce

A fast, discreet cash sale — so both of you can split the proceeds cleanly and move forward.

In most divorces, the house is the biggest asset and the biggest source of friction. A traditional listing means months of coordinating showings, repairs, and price negotiations with someone you may barely be on speaking terms with. A direct cash sale compresses all of that into a few weeks with a certain outcome both attorneys can plan around.

Why divorcing couples choose a cash sale

  • Speed and certainty — a fixed cash number and a fixed closing date make the settlement math simple.
  • Total discretion — no yard sign, no open houses, no neighbors asking questions.
  • No repair negotiations — we buy as-is, so there's nothing to argue about fixing.
  • Neutral and professional — we communicate with both parties (and attorneys) equally and transparently.

We work with your attorneys

Whether the court has ordered the sale or you've both simply agreed it's time, we can coordinate directly with counsel on both sides, provide documentation for the settlement, and time the closing to fit the decree. One less battle — and both of you walk away with your share in cash.
