Jacksonville specifics

Jacksonville and Duval County have shared one consolidated government since October 1, 1968, which is why many county services live under the City of Jacksonville. A few offices are worth knowing if you're dealing with a hard situation:

  • Foreclosure sales. Florida forecloses through the courts. In Duval County, the Clerk of the Circuit Court holds all foreclosure auctions online at duval.realforeclose.com, Monday through Friday, with the first sale at 11:00 a.m. If a sale brings in more than is owed, the owner can file a claim with the clerk for the surplus.
  • Probate. Estate cases go through the circuit court for Florida's Fourth Judicial Circuit and are filed with the Duval County Clerk's Probate Department, which also handles guardianships.
  • Property taxes. The Duval County Tax Collector (taxcollector.jacksonville.gov) collects real estate taxes for the county.
  • Code enforcement. The city's Municipal Code Compliance Division, part of the Neighborhoods Department, enforces property maintenance, unsafe structure and zoning rules. Complaints come in through (904) 630-CITY.

If you've been served in a foreclosure case, talk with your lender, a HUD-approved housing counselor or a Florida attorney about all your options. Selling is one of them, and we never charge a fee to look at yours.

Questions sellers ask

Who buys houses for cash in Jacksonville, FL?
Several companies do, and they don’t all work the same way. MarKey is an Atlanta-based cash buyer that purchases houses across Jacksonville and Duval County, as-is, with no fees or commissions. Send us the address for a written, no-obligation offer.
How fast can I sell my house in Jacksonville?
With a cash buyer there’s no loan approval to wait on, so closing can happen in as little as two weeks when the title is clear. Probate, liens or title issues can stretch that out, and you pick the date.
Where are Duval County foreclosure auctions held?
The Duval County Clerk of the Circuit Court holds foreclosure auctions online at duval.realforeclose.com, Monday through Friday except legal holidays, with the first sale at 11:00 a.m. Your sale date comes from the court’s final judgment, so if you have one, send it to us and we’ll tell you honestly whether there’s time to close.
Can I sell my Jacksonville house if it has code violations?
Yes. We buy houses with open code cases and factor the needed work into our offer, so you don’t have to make repairs first. Fines or liens are typically settled from the sale at closing.
Do I need to clean out my house before selling to a cash buyer?
No. Take anything you want to keep and leave the rest behind. We handle the cleanout after closing.
