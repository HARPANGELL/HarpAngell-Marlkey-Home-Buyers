Chamblee specifics

Chamblee is in DeKalb County. The county seat is Decatur, which is where the court side of a hard sale usually plays out.

  • Foreclosure sales: DeKalb County holds them on the first Tuesday of each month at the courthouse door of the DeKalb County Courthouse, 556 North McDonough Street in Decatur.
  • Probate: The DeKalb County Probate Court is in the Judicial Tower at that same address.
  • Property taxes: Chamblee doesn't mail its own tax bill. City property taxes are billed and collected by the DeKalb County Tax Commissioner along with the county's, and the Tax Commissioner runs a walk-in North Office on Clairmont Road in Chamblee.
  • Code enforcement: Code Enforcement sits in the city's Planning and Development department and handles property maintenance issues such as junk cars, trash, outdoor storage, parking on grass and overcrowding.

Chamblee has grown through annexation, including an area with Huntley Hills in 2010 and Beverly Hills and Beverly Woods in 2013, so city rules now cover streets that used to be unincorporated. The DeKalb County Tax Commissioner bills and collects City of Chamblee property taxes as well as DeKalb County’s. Its North Office is on Clairmont Road in Chamblee. If you’re behind, let us know up front so we can account for it in your numbers.
Do you buy older ranch houses in Chamblee that need updating?
Yes. Whether it’s original wiring, a dated kitchen or a roof near the end of its life, we buy as-is. You don’t have to update anything before you sell.

Often, yes. Chamblee’s Code Enforcement handles issues like junk cars, outdoor storage and parking on grass, and an open case doesn’t automatically rule out a sale. Send us the notice and we’ll give you our honest take.
