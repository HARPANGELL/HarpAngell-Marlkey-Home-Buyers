Lawrenceville specifics

Lawrenceville is the county seat of Gwinnett County, and a lot of what matters in a hard sale happens at one building on Langley Drive.

  • Foreclosure sales: Gwinnett's sales are held on the first Tuesday of the month at the county courthouse, the Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center at 75 Langley Drive, not the historic 1885 courthouse on the square. Under Georgia law, your lender must mail you notice at least 30 days ahead and advertise the sale for four straight weeks.
  • Probate: The Estate Division of the Gwinnett County Probate Court is on the main floor of that same building.
  • Property taxes: The Gwinnett County Tax Commissioner bills county taxes and holds its tax sales in front of the Justice and Administration Center. City taxes are no longer on the county bill; the City of Lawrenceville Finance Department bills them separately.
  • Code enforcement: Inside city limits, the city's Code Enforcement team handles violations of the City Code, Zoning Ordinance and Property Maintenance Ordinance. For a Lawrenceville address outside the city, Gwinnett County Code Enforcement takes over.

Got a notice from the lender, the court or the city? Pass it along with the address, and we'll tell you where things stand.

Questions sellers ask

Where is the Gwinnett County foreclosure auction held?
Gwinnett holds its foreclosure sales on the first Tuesday of the month at the Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive in Lawrenceville. That’s the county courthouse today, not the old courthouse on the square. If your house is on the list, contact us quickly so there’s time to look at a sale.
Can I sell my house in Lawrenceville without making repairs?
Yes. We buy as-is, so you can skip the contractor quotes, the cleanup and the staging. We account for the condition in our offer instead of handing you a repair list.
Do I pay Lawrenceville city taxes separately from Gwinnett County?
For a house within the city, yes. City of Lawrenceville taxes are no longer included on the Gwinnett County bill, and the city’s Finance Department bills them on its own. If either bill is past due, tell us early so we can work it into your numbers.
Can I sell an inherited house in Lawrenceville before probate is finished?
Sometimes. It depends on who has legal authority to sell and what the probate court has approved so far. Your attorney can tell you where the estate stands, and we can work with them on timing.
How quickly do cash home buyers in Lawrenceville close?
The title search and your own plans matter more than anything else. With no buyer’s loan involved, the main wait is on paperwork. Tell us the date you have in mind and we’ll say honestly whether it’s doable.
