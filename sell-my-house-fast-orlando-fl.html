Orlando specifics

  • Foreclosure sales. Florida foreclosures run through the courts. In Orange County, the Clerk of Courts conducts foreclosure auctions online at myorangeclerk.realforeclose.com, a practice the Ninth Judicial Circuit authorized in 2011. The clerk's site also offers foreclosure education resources built with the Orange County Bar Association.
  • Probate. Orange County is part of the Ninth Judicial Circuit, and estate cases are filed through the Orange County Clerk's Probate Division in downtown Orlando, which processes formal and summary administrations, wills and guardianships.
  • Property taxes. The Orange County Tax Collector (octaxcol.com) collects property taxes and has sections for delinquent property taxes and tax certificate and deed sales.
  • Code enforcement. The City of Orlando's Code Enforcement Division, in the Economic Development Department, enforces city codes. Its Code Enforcement Board hears violations, and owners can look up code cases and liens online.

Facing a foreclosure filing? Contact your lender and a HUD-approved housing counselor to understand your choices. Selling may or may not be the right one, and our review never costs you anything.

Questions sellers ask

Who buys houses in Orlando for cash?
Cash buyers like MarKey purchase houses directly from owners, with no agent or bank loan involved. We’re based in Atlanta and buy across Orlando and Orange County in any condition. Send us the address for a written, no-obligation offer.
Where can I find Orange County foreclosure sale dates?
The Orange County Clerk of Courts lists and holds foreclosure auctions online at myorangeclerk.realforeclose.com. Your own sale date is set in the court’s final judgment, so if you have one, send it to us early and we’ll tell you honestly whether a sale could close in time.
Can I sell my house in Orlando with a code enforcement lien?
In many cases, yes. Code liens are typically settled from the sale proceeds at closing, and we factor them into our offer. Tell us what you know about the case and we’ll review it.
How long does it take to sell a house for cash in Orlando?
With no lender involved, a cash sale can close in as little as two weeks when the title is clear. Estates, liens or title issues can take longer, and we work around your timeline.
Do I have to make repairs before selling my Orlando house?
No. We buy as-is, and the condition is part of our offer from the start. You won’t get a repair list after an inspection.
