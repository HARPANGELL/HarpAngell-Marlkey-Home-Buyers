Questions sellers ask

Can I sell a house with foundation problems?
Yes. Foundation issues make a traditional sale harder because many buyers’ lenders won’t finance the house, but a cash buyer can purchase it as-is. Send us any engineer’s report or repair quotes you have, and we’ll factor them into a straight offer.
Will a cash buyer buy a house with fire or water damage?
Yes, we look at fire, smoke and water-damaged houses as-is, with no repairs or cleanup needed. If there’s an open insurance claim, tell us about it up front so we can account for it in the offer.
Can I sell a hoarder house without cleaning it out?
Yes. Take whatever you want to keep and leave everything else, and we handle the cleanout after closing. No judgment, and no need to apologize for the condition.
What do I do if my house failed inspection and the buyer backed out?
The house can still sell. We don’t need a lender’s approval or a repair addendum, and we make our offer after seeing the house as it is. Send us the inspection report and we can usually get back to you within 24 hours.
Should I fix my house before selling or sell it as-is?
It depends on what the repairs cost, how much they’d add to the price, and how much time and cash you have. Big projects like foundation or roof work tie up money and months and don’t always pay back. We’re happy to run the numbers with you either way, with no obligation.
