Behind on Your Mortgage Payments?

Get ahead of the problem — sell for cash, pay off the loan, and protect your credit and your equity.

Missing one payment feels recoverable. By the third, the letters turn threatening, the fees stack up, and foreclosure stops being a distant word. Here's what the bank won't tell you: at this stage you have more leverage than you think — because you still own the house and its equity. Acting early is the difference between choosing your outcome and having one chosen for you.

Your realistic options right now

  • Catch up or modify the loan — works if the hardship was temporary. Ask your lender about forbearance first.
  • List with an agent — can net more, if the house shows well and you can float payments for 2–3 more months.
  • Sell fast for cash — stops the bleeding now: the loan gets paid off at closing and late fees stop accruing.
  • Do nothing — the only truly bad option. Fees compound, equity erodes, and foreclosure becomes inevitable.

What a fast sale actually does for you

The moment we close, your mortgage is paid off in full — missed payments, fees and all — and whatever equity remains goes to you in cash. The late marks on your credit stop accumulating, and you avoid the foreclosure entry that would follow you for seven years. Tell us where things stand and we'll show you your numbers, judgment-free.
