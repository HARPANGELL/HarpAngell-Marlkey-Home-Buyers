Questions sellers ask

What happens when you miss a mortgage payment?
Most loans charge a late fee after a grace period, and once you’re 30 days behind, the missed payment can be reported to the credit bureaus. Your servicer is also required to reach out early about your options. The sooner you act, the more choices you have.
How many mortgage payments can you miss before foreclosure?
Federal rules generally keep a servicer from starting foreclosure until you’re more than 120 days behind, which is roughly four missed payments. In Georgia, Texas and Tennessee, a sale can follow quickly once foreclosure starts, so don’t wait for that point. If you’re already a few months behind, tell us where things stand and we’ll show you your options.
Who can help me if I’m behind on my mortgage?
Start with your lender and ask about forbearance, a repayment plan or a loan modification. A free HUD-approved housing counselor can also go over your options with you; HUD’s line is 800-569-4287. If selling is the better path, we’ll give you a no-obligation cash offer so you can compare.
Can I sell my house if I’m behind on mortgage payments?
Yes. The missed payments, fees and loan balance are paid off from the sale at closing, and whatever is left goes to you. If you owe more than the house can sell for, the lender has to approve the sale, and we’ll tell you up front if that’s where you are.
Will selling my house hurt my credit if I’m behind on payments?
Late payments already reported stay on your credit report, but a sale that pays off the loan stops new late marks and avoids a foreclosure on your record. That can make a real difference when you apply for credit or a rental later. Tell us where things stand, and we’ll walk you through the numbers.
