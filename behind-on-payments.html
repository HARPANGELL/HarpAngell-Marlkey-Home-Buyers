It happens to good people

A job loss, a divorce, a medical bill, a rate adjustment, one bad year. Falling behind on a mortgage is far more common than people admit, and the stress makes it easy to avoid opening the mail. But the months you're behind are the months you have the most choices. Waiting is the one thing that only makes it harder.

What happens as the months go by

How far behindWhat usually happens
1 paymentLate fee after the grace period. Calls and letters from the servicer.
2-3 paymentsReported late to the credit bureaus. A breach or demand letter may arrive.
About 4 payments (120+ days)Federal rules generally allow the servicer to start foreclosure.
After thatThe state's foreclosure process runs. In Georgia, Texas and Tennessee it can move in weeks; in Florida it goes through court.

Your options, in one line each

  • Call your servicer: ask about a repayment plan, forbearance, or loan modification.
  • Talk to a free HUD-approved housing counselor: they can help you prepare.
  • Refinance: if your credit and equity still allow it.
  • Sell the house: before it reaches foreclosure, while you still control the price and timing.
  • Short sale: if you owe more than the house is worth, with the lender's approval.

When selling makes the most sense

Selling is worth a serious look if the payment no longer fits your life, if you've tried a workout and it didn't stick, if the house needs repairs you can't afford, or if you'd rather protect your credit and your equity than spend the next year fighting to keep up. A sale before foreclosure that covers the payoff pays the loan in full and lets you move on with any money left over.

Frequently asked questions

How many mortgage payments can I miss before foreclosure?
Federal rules generally keep servicers from starting foreclosure until you're more than 120 days behind, roughly four payments. After that, your state's process controls the timeline.
Can I sell my house if I'm behind on payments?
Yes. The past-due amount and the loan are paid from the sale at closing. Selling early, before a foreclosure sale is scheduled, gives you the most time and the most money.
I can't afford my house anymore. What are my options?
Talk to your servicer about a workout, talk to a free HUD-approved counselor, or sell the house on your terms. We can tell you what a cash sale would leave you with, usually within 24 hours.
Can I sell my house if I owe more than it's worth?
Possibly, through a short sale that the lender has to approve. Tell us what you owe and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
Will selling hurt my credit?
The missed payments already reported stay on your report, but a sale that pays the loan in full avoids a completed foreclosure, which is far more damaging.
