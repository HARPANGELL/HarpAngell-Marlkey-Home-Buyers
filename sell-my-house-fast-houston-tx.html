Houston specifics

Houston is the county seat of Harris County, and nearly all of the city sits inside it (small parts reach into Fort Bend and Montgomery counties). Here's where the local pieces of a hard sale usually happen.

  • Foreclosure sales: Harris County holds its sales on the first Tuesday of the month at the Bayou City Event Center, 9401 Knight Road, not at the courthouse. Notices of trustee sale are filed and posted with the Harris County Clerk 21 days before the auction.
  • Probate: Estates are heard by the Harris County Probate Courts, five courts numbered 1 through 5, all in downtown Houston.
  • Property taxes: The Harris County Tax Office, run by the county Tax Assessor-Collector, handles tax bills, delinquent accounts and installment payment plans.
  • Code enforcement: The city's Community Code Enforcement division, formerly Inspections and Public Service under the Department of Neighborhoods, has moved to Houston Public Works.

Have a sale date, a probate case or a letter from the city? Send it along with your address so we can see where things stand.

Questions sellers ask

How fast can I sell my house in Houston?
It mostly comes down to the title work and the date you want. Because we pay cash, there’s no buyer’s loan to wait on, which is often what drags a sale out. Tell us your deadline and we’ll be honest about whether we can meet it.
Where are foreclosure auctions held in Harris County?
Harris County holds its foreclosure sales on the first Tuesday of the month at the Bayou City Event Center, 9401 Knight Road in Houston. Notices of sale are filed with the County Clerk 21 days ahead. If your house is on the list, reach out right away so there’s time to look at a sale.
Do you buy houses in Houston that need a lot of work?
Yes. We buy as-is, whether it’s an old roof, flood or water damage, or a house that needs a full renovation. You don’t have to fix or clean anything before you sell.
Can I sell a house in probate in Houston?
Often, yes, once the executor or administrator has the court’s authority to sell. In Harris County, estates go through one of the five county probate courts downtown. We can work with your attorney and the title company on timing.
Do cash home buyers in Houston charge fees?
We don’t. MarKey charges no commissions or fees and pays typical closing costs. You’ll see what you’d walk away with before you agree to anything.
