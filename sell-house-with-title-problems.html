What "curative title" means

Curative title is the work of fixing the problems in a property's chain of title so a title company will insure it. Depending on the problem, that can mean locating heirs and getting signatures, recording releases, paying off liens, correcting deeds, or going to court to settle ownership. It takes know-how, time, and usually an attorney, which is exactly why most buyers walk away. We don't.

Your options, in one line each

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell a house with a title problem?
Often yes, but not through a normal sale until the problem is fixed. We buy houses with title problems and work with title attorneys to do the curative work.
The house is still in my deceased parent's name. Can I sell it?
Usually, once someone has legal authority to sell, through probate or your state's heirship process. We can explain the options in your state and buy the house as part of the process.
What if one of the heirs won't sign or can't be found?
There are legal ways to deal with missing or uncooperative co-owners, but they vary by state and take time. Tell us the situation and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.
How long does curative title work take?
It depends on the problem. A missing lien release can take a few weeks; a court case to settle ownership can take months. We'll give you a realistic timeline after we look at the title.
Do you charge for looking at my title problem?
No. We review it for free and make an offer with no obligation.
