Is Your House Going to Auction? What You Can Still Do

A scheduled foreclosure or tax sale is the last stop, but it isn't over until the sale happens. Here's what to do with the time you have left.

Seeing your house listed for auction is terrifying. Don't freeze.

Maybe you found the notice in the paper, online, or taped to the door. Maybe a stranger knocked and asked if the house is for sale. Either way, it means the sale is close. The worst thing you can do now is wait and hope. The best thing you can do is find out, today, what's still possible.

What happens at the auction

The house is sold to the highest bidder, often investors who have never seen the inside. The opening bid is usually based on what's owed, not what the house is worth. You don't control the price or the date. If the house sells for more than the debt, the extra may be held as surplus or excess funds that you have to claim, which can take months. If nobody outbids the lender, the lender takes the house.

Your options before the sale, in one line each

  • Pay what's owed: catch up on the mortgage or pay the taxes before the sale.
  • Ask for time: the lender or county may agree to a plan; get anything agreed in writing.
  • Sell before the sale date: the debt is paid from the proceeds at closing.
  • Get legal help: a real estate or bankruptcy attorney can tell you about other options.

For a mortgage foreclosure, also call your servicer and a free HUD-approved housing counselor. If you're not sure which path fits, send us the address and we'll walk you through it.

Why a sale before the auction usually leaves you better off

A sale you choose means you control the price, the closing date, and when you move. The debt is paid in full, and the rest of the money goes to you at closing instead of sitting in a surplus account or going to a bidder. And you avoid having a completed foreclosure or tax sale attached to your name.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell my house if it's already scheduled for auction?
Usually yes, until the sale happens. The debt is paid from the sale at closing. The question is whether a sale can close in time, which is why we ask for your auction date right away.
How fast can you close before a foreclosure auction?
When the title is clear, we can close in as little as 14 days, sometimes faster. If the date is very close, call us and we'll tell you honestly whether it's realistic.
What happens if my house sells at auction for more than I owe?
The extra is usually held as surplus or excess funds that you can claim, but it can take time and paperwork. Selling before the auction puts that money in your hands at closing.
Can I get my house back after a tax sale?
In some states you can redeem the property for a period after a tax sale by paying the amount due plus penalties. The rules vary by state and by homestead status. Your state guide explains them.
Will you still buy the house if it needs a lot of work?
Yes. We buy houses as-is, including houses that need major repairs, are vacant, or have tenants.
