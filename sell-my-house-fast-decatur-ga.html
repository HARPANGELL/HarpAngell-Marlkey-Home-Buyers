Decatur specifics

Decatur is the county seat of DeKalb County, so the courthouse, the probate court and the county's foreclosure sales are all downtown.

  • Foreclosure sales: On the first Tuesday of every month, DeKalb County's sales take place at the courthouse door of the DeKalb County Courthouse, 556 North McDonough Street. Before that, your lender has to send notice at least 30 days ahead and advertise the sale weekly for four weeks.
  • Probate: Estates go through the DeKalb County Probate Court, in the Judicial Tower at the same McDonough Street address.
  • Property taxes: Decatur handles city taxes itself. The City of Decatur mails its own bills twice a year, based on values set by the county, while county taxes come from the DeKalb County Tax Commissioner.
  • Code enforcement: The city's Design, Environment and Construction division houses code enforcement, covering code violations, illegal signs, graffiti and tall weeds or grass.
  • Historic districts: The city has five local historic districts: MAK, Clairemont Avenue, Ponce de Leon Court, Parkwood and Old Decatur. Work on homes there may need a Certificate of Appropriateness from the Historic Preservation Commission.

Got a sale notice or a letter from the city? Send it over with the address and we'll tell you what we see.

Questions sellers ask

Where are foreclosure sales held in DeKalb County?
DeKalb’s foreclosure auctions happen on the first Tuesday of each month at the courthouse door on North McDonough Street, right in downtown Decatur. If you’ve received a notice of sale, reach out right away so there’s time to see whether a sale can close first.
Does the City of Decatur or DeKalb County send my property tax bill?
Both, if you live inside the city. The City of Decatur bills its own taxes twice a year using values from the county, and the DeKalb County Tax Commissioner bills county taxes. Let us know if either is past due so we can include it in your numbers.
Do you buy historic homes in Decatur that need work?
Yes. We buy older homes in places like the MAK and Ponce de Leon Court districts as-is. You don’t have to take on repairs or historic review before you sell.
Can I sell an inherited house in Decatur if there are several heirs?
It’s often possible, but it depends on who has legal authority to sell, which can mean going through the DeKalb County Probate Court first. Your attorney can explain the options for your family. We’re happy to work with everyone involved on timing.
What happens after I ask for a cash offer on my Decatur house?
We’ll ask a few questions about the house and your timeline, then look at the property. Most sellers get a written cash offer within a day. You can take it, ask questions or walk away at no cost.
