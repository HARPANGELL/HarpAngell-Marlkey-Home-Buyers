Where things happen in Gwinnett County

If your sale is tied to a foreclosure notice, an estate, back taxes or a code letter, these are the offices involved. Lawrenceville is the county seat.

  • Foreclosure sales: On the first Tuesday of the month, between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., in front of the Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive, Lawrenceville.
  • Where sales are advertised: The Gwinnett Daily Post. Georgia requires the ad to run once a week for four weeks before the sale, and you must be mailed notice at least 30 days ahead.
  • Probate: Gwinnett County Probate Court, Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive, Lawrenceville (court website).
  • Property taxes: Gwinnett County Tax Commissioner (gwinnetttaxcommissioner.com).
  • Code enforcement: In unincorporated Gwinnett, the county’s Code Enforcement Division (770-513-5020). Cities run their own.
  • Free legal help: Atlanta Legal Aid Society’s Gwinnett office, 678-376-4545.

Statewide, you can also search foreclosure ads on Georgia Public Notice. A free HUD-approved housing counselor is at 800-569-4287.

Questions sellers ask

Where are foreclosure sales held in Gwinnett County?
On the first Tuesday of the month, between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., in front of the Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive, Lawrenceville. If you have a sale date, send it to us with your address and we’ll tell you honestly whether there’s time for a sale to close first.
Where is the Gwinnett County probate court?
Gwinnett County Probate Court, Gwinnett Justice and Administration Center, 75 Langley Drive, Lawrenceville. If you’re handling an estate, the house can usually be sold once the personal representative has authority.
Where are Gwinnett County foreclosure notices published?
In The Gwinnett Daily Post, the county’s legal organ, once a week for the four weeks before the sale. You can also search statewide on Georgia Public Notice. Your lender must also mail you notice at least 30 days before the sale.
Who handles property taxes in Gwinnett County?
Gwinnett County Tax Commissioner (gwinnetttaxcommissioner.com). If back taxes are part of why you’re selling, they’re paid off from the sale proceeds at closing, like any other lien.
