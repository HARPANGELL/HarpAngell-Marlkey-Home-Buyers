Maybe the house needs more work than you have the time, money or energy for. Maybe it's still full of furniture. Or maybe you just don't want strangers walking through it every weekend. Selling as-is means you sell the house exactly the way it sits today, and the buyer takes on the rest.

That's what we do. MarKey Home Buyers is an Atlanta company that buys houses directly from owners, in whatever condition they're in, across metro Atlanta and Georgia, and in Florida, Texas and Tennessee too.

What as-is means when you sell to us

  • No repairs. Leaky roof, tired HVAC, a dated kitchen, a long list from a buyer's inspection. You don't fix any of it.
  • No cleaning. Take what you want to keep and leave the rest. We handle the cleanout after closing.
  • No showings. No open houses, no lockbox, no keeping the place spotless for weeks. Just one short walkthrough.
  • No repair requests later. We see the house before we make our offer, so its condition is already part of the number.
  • No fees or commissions. We're not agents, and we pay typical closing costs.

Is selling as-is the right move for you?

Listing with an agent can bring a higher price when a house is updated and shows well. If that's your house, we'll tell you. Selling as-is to a cash buyer usually makes the most sense when:

  • The house needs repairs you can't or don't want to pay for
  • You're on a deadline, or you want a firm closing date you can plan around
  • The house is inherited, vacant, a rental with tenants, or far from where you live
  • You'd rather skip showings, inspections and waiting on a buyer's loan
  • The house has already been turned down, or a sale fell through

Questions sellers ask

Can I sell my house as-is in Georgia?
Yes. Selling as-is means the buyer takes the house in its current condition, without asking you to make repairs. You should still be upfront about problems you know about, and we’d rather hear about them early so our offer accounts for them.
Do I have to clean out my house before selling to a cash buyer?
Not with us. Take anything you want to keep and leave the rest, and we handle the cleanout after closing. That includes houses that are still full of furniture and belongings.
How much do cash buyers pay for a house sold as-is?
It depends on the house. We start with what it would likely sell for after repairs, subtract the cost of the work and our costs, and show you that math. If listing with an agent would put more in your pocket, we’ll tell you.
How fast can I sell my house as-is in Atlanta?
Once you accept our offer, closing can happen in as little as two weeks when the title is clear. If you need more time to move, you pick a later date. Tell us your timeline and we’ll work around it.
Are there fees when you sell a house as-is to a cash buyer?
There shouldn’t be, and with us there aren’t. We don’t charge fees or commissions, and we pay typical closing costs. Any mortgage or liens are paid from the sale at closing, and the rest goes to you.
