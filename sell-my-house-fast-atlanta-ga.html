Atlanta specifics

Most of Atlanta lies in Fulton County, and part of the city’s east side lies in DeKalb County. Which county your house is in decides where its paperwork lives:

  • Foreclosure sales: the first Tuesday of the month, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Fulton sells on the courthouse steps at 136 Pryor Street SW downtown; DeKalb sells on the steps of the DeKalb County Courthouse, 556 N. McDonough Street, Decatur.
  • Where sales are advertised: the South Fulton Neighbor for Fulton (it replaced the Daily Report as Fulton’s legal organ in January 2024) and The Champion for DeKalb. You can also search Georgia Public Notice.
  • Probate: Fulton County Probate Court, 136 Pryor Street SW, 2nd floor; DeKalb County Probate Court, Judicial Tower, 556 N. McDonough Street, Decatur.
  • County property taxes: the Fulton County Tax Commissioner or the DeKalb County Tax Commissioner.
  • City code violations: reported and tracked through ATL311, 404-546-0311 or atl311.com.
  • Free help before you decide: Atlanta Legal Aid Society’s Home Defense Program (Fulton office 404-524-5811, DeKalb office 404-377-0701) and a free HUD-approved housing counselor at 800-569-4287.

Questions sellers ask

How fast can I sell my house in Atlanta?
It mostly comes down to the title work and the date you want. Because we pay cash, there’s no buyer’s loan or appraisal to wait on. When title is clear, we can close in as little as two weeks through a Georgia closing attorney, or later if you need time to move.
Do you buy Atlanta houses that need a lot of work?
Yes. We buy as-is: old roofs, foundation problems, fire or water damage, code violations, or a house full of belongings. You don’t fix or clean anything.
What does selling to a cash buyer in Atlanta cost me?
We don’t charge commissions or fees, and we cover typical closing costs. Your own mortgage payoff, liens and prorated property taxes come out of the proceeds, as they would with any buyer, and you’ll see the estimated settlement statement before closing.
Where are foreclosure sales held for Atlanta homes?
It depends on the county. Most of Atlanta is in Fulton County, where sales are held at the Fulton County Courthouse, 136 Pryor Street SW. The part of Atlanta in DeKalb County sells at the DeKalb County Courthouse, 556 N. McDonough Street in Decatur. Both happen on the first Tuesday of the month.
Is a cash offer lower than listing with an agent?
Usually, yes. In return you skip repairs, showings, commissions and months of carrying costs. If your house is in good shape and you have time, listing may net you more, and we’ll tell you so. Here’s the honest math: cash offer vs. listing with an agent.
