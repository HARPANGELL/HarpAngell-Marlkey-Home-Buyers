SMS Policy & Opt-Out

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This page describes the MarKey Home Buyers text messaging (SMS) program — what you'll receive, how you opted in, and exactly how to opt out at any time.

About our text messages

MarKey Home Buyers sends conversational and informational text messages regarding your inquiry, property evaluation, appointment scheduling, and account-related updates. We text you only in connection with a request you made — we do not send unsolicited marketing blasts. Message frequency may vary depending on where you are in the process. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.

How you opt in

You receive texts from us only if you checked the SMS consent box when submitting a form on this website, or if you texted us first at (678) 926-9776. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or of receiving a cash offer — if you prefer calls or email only, just tell us.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any text message from us and you will be immediately unsubscribed; you'll receive one final message confirming your opt-out, and nothing further. You can also opt out by emailing contact@markeyhomebuyers.com or calling (678) 926-9776 and asking us to stop texting you.

Need help?

Reply HELP to any message, email contact@markeyhomebuyers.com, or call/text (678) 926-9776.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our full Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
