Selling a House in Probate, Made Simple

We work with executors, administrators, and heirs to turn estate property into cash — without repairs, cleanouts, or drama.

When a loved one passes, the last thing most families want to manage is a house: the taxes, the insurance, the upkeep, the memories in every closet. Probate adds courts and paperwork on top of grief. We've helped Atlanta families through this many times, and we've built our process to be as gentle and simple as the situation allows.

How we make probate sales easier

  • We buy completely as-is — take what you want from the home and leave the rest. We handle all cleanout.
  • We work alongside your probate attorney and the court's timeline — no pressure to close before the estate is ready.
  • One cash buyer, no showings — no parade of strangers through your family's home.
  • Fair, transparent offers — we'll show you how we arrived at our number, and heirs can verify everything.

Don't have an attorney yet?

No problem. We can point you toward experienced local probate attorneys and explain the typical steps — whether the estate needs full probate, or qualifies for a simpler path. Reach out and we'll help you get oriented, with no obligation to sell to us at all.

No pressure, ever. Our offer is free and comes with zero obligation. Even if we're not the right fit, we'll point you in the right direction.

How it works

Tell us about the property, get a fair cash offer within 24 hours, and close on the date you choose — in as little as two weeks. We buy as-is, pay typical closing costs, and never charge fees or commissions.
