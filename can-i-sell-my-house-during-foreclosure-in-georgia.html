Can I Sell My House During Foreclosure in Georgia?

Straight answers from Georgia home buyers who deal with this every week.

Yes. Right up until the moment the auctioneer's hammer falls, the house is still yours — and selling it is often the single best move for protecting your equity and your credit.

Why selling during foreclosure works

When you sell before the auction, the sale proceeds pay off the mortgage — including the missed payments, late fees, and the lender's legal costs — and everything above that number belongs to you. Compare that to an auction, where your house is typically bid on by investors looking for a discount, and where anything can go wrong for your bottom line.

The catch: the clock

Georgia's timeline is unforgiving. Once the notice of sale goes out, you may have only 30–45 days. A financed buyer's mortgage takes 30–45 days to close when everything goes right — one appraisal hiccup and you miss the auction. That's why sales during foreclosure are usually cash sales: no loan approval, no appraisal contingency, closing in as little as two weeks.
