San Antonio specifics

San Antonio is the county seat of Bexar County, and much of the county's court business happens at one downtown address: 100 Dolorosa.

  • Foreclosure sales: According to Bexar County, sales take place on the west side of the Bexar County Courthouse, 100 Dolorosa, on the first Tuesday of every month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. When that Tuesday lands on January 1 or July 4, the sale moves to the first Wednesday.
  • Probate: Bexar County has three probate courts, Probate Courts 1, 2 and 3, also at 100 Dolorosa.
  • Property taxes: The Bexar County Tax Assessor-Collector collects and disburses property taxes for 66 separate taxing jurisdictions.
  • Code enforcement: The City of San Antonio's Code Enforcement division sits within the Development Services Department. It issues notices and citations, and it can file liens and clean up violations itself when it has to.

A lien from the city or a sale date from the county can change the timeline. Mention it when you contact us, and we'll factor it in.

Questions sellers ask

How fast can I sell my house in San Antonio?
You pick the date. With a cash sale there’s no buyer financing to wait on, so timing mostly depends on the title search. If you’re up against a deadline, tell us on the first call.
Where are foreclosure sales held in Bexar County?
Bexar County sales take place on the west side of the Bexar County Courthouse at 100 Dolorosa in San Antonio. They run on the first Tuesday of the month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., moving to Wednesday when that Tuesday is January 1 or July 4. If you have a sale date, call us as early as you can.
Can I sell my San Antonio house if I’m behind on property taxes?
Usually, yes. Back taxes are normally paid out of the sale proceeds at closing. Send us what you owe and we’ll show you how that affects your bottom line.
Do you buy inherited houses in San Antonio?
Yes. If the estate is still open, it’s handled by one of Bexar County’s probate courts at 100 Dolorosa, and someone with authority from the court usually needs to sign. We can coordinate with your attorney and the title company.
Are cash home buyers in San Antonio legit?
It’s smart to check before you sign anything. A real buyer explains the offer, closes through a title company and never asks you for money up front. We charge no fees, and you can take as long as you need to decide.
