Texas at a glance

Texas rules move faster than many homeowners expect, which is why the timing of a sale matters so much here.

TopicHow it works in Texas
Foreclosure typeMostly non-judicial trustee sales under Tex. Prop. Code § 51.002; home equity loans need a court order (Tex. Const. art. XVI, § 50)
Required noticesNotice of default with at least 20 days to cure, then a notice of sale at least 21 days before the sale
Sale day and placeFirst Tuesday of the month, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at the county courthouse or an area the commissioners court designates
Federal 120-day ruleThe first foreclosure notice or filing generally can't come until you're more than 120 days behind (12 CFR 1024.41(f))
Property taxesDelinquent if not paid before February 1; penalty reaches 12% by July 1, plus 1% interest a month (Tex. Tax Code §§ 31.02, 33.01)
Tax salesAfter a delinquent tax suit, a sheriff's or constable's sale, also on the first Tuesday or online (§ 34.01)
Redemption after a tax sale2 years for a homestead (25% or 50% premium); 180 days for most other property (§ 34.21)
Redemption after a mortgage saleGenerally none

Why sellers choose a cash sale

We'll be straight with you: if your house is in great shape and you have months to spare, listing with a good agent may bring a higher price. A cash sale makes sense when other things matter more:

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy houses anywhere in Texas?
We focus on the Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio and Austin metro areas. If your house is elsewhere in Texas, send us the address anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we can make an offer. We usually respond within 24 hours.
How fast can you buy my house in Texas?
We can close in as little as 14 days once the title is ready, or on a later date you choose. Most of that time is the title company confirming payoffs and clearing the title. We'll give you a realistic closing date with our offer.
Do you charge fees or commissions?
No. We charge no fees or commissions, and we pay typical closing costs. Mortgage payoffs, back taxes and liens are paid from the sale price at closing, and you see all of it in writing before you sign.
Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?
No. We buy houses as-is, including houses with damage, code violations or a lot of stuff left inside. Take what you want and leave the rest. Send us the address and we'll make an offer on the house as it stands today.
Are you real estate agents?
No. We're real estate investors making offers to buy your house directly, not agents representing you. Depending on the house, we buy it ourselves or assign our purchase contract to another investor buyer, and we tell you which in writing before you sign. You're always free to talk with a real estate attorney first.
Can you buy my house if I'm behind on the mortgage or taxes?
Yes. The title company pays the mortgage, missed payments and back taxes from the sale at closing. If a sale date is posted, tell us right away so we can tell you whether there's time to close before it.
