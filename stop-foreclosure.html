Stop Foreclosure Before It's Too Late

A fast cash sale can halt the auction, protect your remaining equity, and limit the damage to your credit.

Falling behind with the bank is more common than most people realize — a job loss, medical bills, or one hard year is all it takes. But Georgia is a fast foreclosure state: once the bank starts the process, an auction can happen in as little as 30–60 days. The worst thing you can do is wait. The good news: until the day of the sale, you still have options — and selling for cash is often the strongest one.

Why homeowners in pre-foreclosure sell to us

  • Speed — we can close before the auction date, paying off the bank and stopping the foreclosure entirely.
  • Keep your equity — at auction, your equity often evaporates. Selling first means the difference goes to you, not the bank.
  • Protect your credit — a sale hurts far less than a completed foreclosure, which can follow you for seven years.
  • No cost to you — no fees, no commissions, no repairs, and we handle the paperwork and payoff coordination.

Time matters more than anything

Every week that passes narrows your options. Reach out today — even if you just want to understand where you stand. We'll lay out exactly what a sale would look like, what you'd walk away with, and how fast we can move. If keeping the house is a better path for you, we'll tell you that too.

No pressure, ever. Our offer is free and comes with zero obligation. Even if we're not the right fit, we'll point you in the right direction.

Tell us about the property, get a fair cash offer within 24 hours, and close on the date you choose — in as little as two weeks. We buy as-is, pay typical closing costs, and never charge fees or commissions.
