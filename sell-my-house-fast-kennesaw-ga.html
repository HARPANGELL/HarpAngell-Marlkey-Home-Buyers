Kennesaw specifics

Kennesaw sits in Cobb County. The county-level pieces of a sale run through offices in Marietta, while the city handles its own property taxes and code rules.

  • Foreclosure sales: Cobb County's auctions happen on the first Tuesday of each month at the county courthouse in Marietta, not in Kennesaw.
  • Probate: Estates for Kennesaw homeowners are filed with the Cobb County Probate Court on Waddell Street in Marietta.
  • Property taxes: Inside city limits, you get two bills. The Cobb County Tax Commissioner collects county taxes, and the City of Kennesaw Finance Department bills city taxes, which are due December 1. The city holds its own tax sales on the first Tuesday in November at City Hall.
  • Code enforcement: The city's Code Enforcement Division enforces Kennesaw's ordinances, housing codes and zoning codes. Outside city limits, Cobb County's Code Enforcement, part of its Community Development Agency, handles it instead.

Behind on either tax bill, or holding a notice from the city? Send it to us with the address and we'll take a look.
