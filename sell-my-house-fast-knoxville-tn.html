Knoxville specifics

  • Foreclosure. Tennessee home foreclosures mostly happen without a lawsuit. Current state law requires at least two newspaper notices, the first at least 20 days ahead of the sale, plus at least 20 continuous days of online posting. Some deeds of trust still call for three newspaper ads, so read yours.
  • Probate. In Knox County, estates go through the Probate Division of the Knox County Chancery Court, led by the Clerk and Master. It admits wills to probate, appoints and supervises personal representatives, and issues letters testamentary or letters of administration.
  • Property taxes. The Knox County Trustee (trustee.knoxcounty.org) collects property taxes and posts tax sale information on its site.
  • Codes enforcement. The City of Knoxville's Neighborhood Codes Enforcement office takes complaints through the 311 Center at 865-215-4311, and a code enforcement officer follows up with an inspection.

If a notice of sale has arrived, call your lender about reinstatement or a payment plan and consider a HUD-approved housing counselor. If selling looks like your best path, send us the sale date and we'll be straight with you about the timing.

Who buys houses for cash in Knoxville, TN?
Several companies do, and it’s worth comparing how they work. MarKey is an Atlanta-based cash buyer that purchases Knoxville and Knox County houses as-is, with no fees or commissions. We put every offer in writing.
Which court handles probate in Knox County?
In Knox County, probate is handled by the Probate Division of the Knox County Chancery Court, under the Clerk and Master. Once the estate has a personal representative with authority to sell, we can make an offer and work with the estate’s attorney on closing.
How fast can I sell my house in Knoxville?
With cash and no lender, we can close in as little as two weeks when the title is clear. If you need time to move or sort out an estate, we’ll set a later date.
Can I sell a house in Knoxville that needs major repairs?

What if my Knoxville house has a codes complaint?
