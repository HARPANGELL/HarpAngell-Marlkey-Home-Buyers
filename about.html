About MarKey Home Buyers

A local Atlanta home-buying company built on straight answers, fair offers, and doing what we say we'll do.

MarKey Home Buyers is a locally owned real estate company that buys houses directly from homeowners across Metro Atlanta. No agents, no listings, no fees — just a straightforward cash sale on your timeline.

We started this company because we watched too many homeowners get squeezed: agents demanding repairs before listing, wholesalers making offers they never intended to honor, and banks running out the clock on families in trouble. We believed there was room for a home buyer that operates the way people actually want to be treated — with honesty about the numbers and respect for the situation.

What we believe

  • An offer should be explained, not just presented. We'll walk you through exactly how we got to our number.
  • No is a fine answer. Our offers come with zero pressure and zero obligation — and if listing with an agent would truly net you more, we'll say so.
  • Every situation deserves dignity. Foreclosure, divorce, inherited homes — we've helped people through all of it, discreetly and without judgment.
  • Local matters. We live here, we buy here, and we know what Atlanta neighborhoods are actually worth.

Meet the team

MarKey Home Buyers is run by its two founders — when you call, you get one of them, not a call center.

William Harp — Co-Founder

William handles much of the day-to-day with sellers, and it shows: he's the guy who will walk you through exactly how an offer was calculated and tell you honestly if listing with an agent would net you more. He's helped Georgia homeowners through foreclosure timelines, inherited houses full of memories, and properties that just needed too much work to sell the normal way.

Peter Register — Co-Founder

Peter keeps every deal moving — coordinating with closing attorneys, title companies, and lenders so sellers get paid on the date they were promised. He's the reason MarKey can close in two weeks when a seller needs it, without the deal falling apart at the finish line.

Together, William and Peter bring 20+ combined years of real estate experience across Metro Atlanta and Georgia — buying, renovating, renting, and above all, helping people find a way forward when a house has become a burden.
