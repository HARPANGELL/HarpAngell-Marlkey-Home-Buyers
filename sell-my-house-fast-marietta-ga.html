Marietta specifics

Marietta is the county seat of Cobb County, so most of the county offices that come into play in a hard sale are right here in town.

  • Foreclosure sales: Like every Georgia county, Cobb holds its sales on the first Tuesday of the month. They take place at the Cobb County courthouse in Marietta, and the notices run in the Marietta Daily Journal, the county's official legal newspaper. Georgia lenders have to mail notice at least 30 days before the sale and advertise it once a week for four weeks.
  • Probate: Estates are handled by the Cobb County Probate Court at 32 Waddell Street in Marietta.
  • Property taxes: The Cobb County Tax Commissioner bills county taxes. Inside city limits, the City of Marietta Tax Division sends its own separate bill, due October 31.
  • Code enforcement: The city's Code Enforcement Division, part of Development Services on Lawrence Street, enforces the housing code, zoning and historic district rules, but only inside Marietta city limits.

Holding a sale notice, a probate case number or a letter from the city? Send it with your address so we can see where things stand.
