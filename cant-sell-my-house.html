Questions sellers ask

Why won’t my house sell?
Common reasons include major repairs, a failed inspection, a price that doesn’t match the condition, title or lien problems, or tenant and vacancy issues. Often the real issue is that most buyers need a loan, and lenders won’t finance the house as it is. Tell us what’s happening and we’ll give you an honest read.
What can I do if my house won’t sell?
You can lower the price, make repairs, change your marketing or agent, or sell directly to a cash buyer who takes the house as-is. The right move depends on how much time and money you can put in. We’ll review your house for free and tell you whether a cash sale makes sense.
Can I sell my house after the buyer’s financing fell through?
Yes. If your contract fell apart over financing or an appraisal, a cash buyer doesn’t need a lender’s approval. Send us the address and we can usually give you a straight answer within 24 hours.
Will anyone buy a house in bad condition?
Yes. Many cash buyers focus on houses that need work, and we buy houses as-is. You don’t need to repair or clean anything before we make an offer.
Can I sell to a cash buyer if my house is listed with a realtor?
Check your listing agreement first, because it may cover a sale to any buyer during the listing period. Many agents are glad to bring a cash offer to a listing that has stalled, and realtors are welcome to refer deals to us directly.
