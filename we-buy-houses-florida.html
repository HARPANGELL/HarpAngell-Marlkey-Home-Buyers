Why Florida sellers choose a cash sale

  • As-is means as-is. No repairs, no cleaning, no showings, no open houses.

Frequently asked questions

How fast can I sell my house for cash in Florida?
We usually make an offer within 24 hours and can close in as little as 14 days when title is clear. If you need more time to move, you pick a later date. Title problems, probate or liens can add time, and we'll tell you up front if they do.
Do you charge fees or commissions?
No. We don't charge fees or commissions, and we pay typical closing costs. The number we offer is the price we pay, less any mortgage, taxes or liens paid off from the sale at closing.
Do I have to make repairs before selling my Florida house?
No. We buy as-is, including houses that need major work. Florida sellers still have to disclose known defects that materially affect value, plus the state's flood disclosure, and we'll walk you through that.
Can you buy my house if it's in foreclosure or has liens?
Usually, yes. The mortgage payoff, back taxes and recorded liens are paid from the sale at closing. A sale that closes before a foreclosure auction pays off the loan, so the sale doesn't go forward, as long as there's time to close.
Where in Florida do you buy houses?
We buy in Florida's major metros: Jacksonville, Tampa-St. Petersburg, Orlando, Miami and Fort Lauderdale, along with their counties. If you're nearby, send the address and we'll tell you whether it's a fit.
Is there any obligation if I ask for an offer?
None. Asking for an offer is free and confidential, and you can compare it with other options, including listing with an agent. You keep ownership until closing, and nothing is signed over before then.
