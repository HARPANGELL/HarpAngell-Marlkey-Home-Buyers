Fort Worth specifics

Fort Worth is the county seat of Tarrant County, and two of the things sellers ask us about most happen in one building: the Tarrant County Courthouse at 100 West Weatherford Street.

  • Foreclosure sales: The Tarrant County Clerk says sales take place at the base of the courthouse steps on the west side of that building, on the first Tuesday of every month. The time for each sale is listed on its notice.
  • Probate: Tarrant County's two probate courts, Probate Court No. 1 and No. 2, are inside the same courthouse.
  • Property taxes: Tax accounts, payments and balances owed are handled by the Tarrant County Tax Assessor-Collector.
  • Code enforcement: The City of Fort Worth's Code Compliance department inspects substandard buildings and investigates high grass and weeds, open and vacant structures, and junk vehicles.

Already received a notice from the county or the city? Keep it handy when you reach out, since the dates on it shape what's possible.

Questions sellers ask

How fast can I sell my house in Fort Worth?
Since we pay cash, you’re not waiting on a buyer’s loan approval, so the biggest variable is the title work. Let us know your timeline, and we’ll be upfront about what’s possible.
Where are foreclosure sales held in Tarrant County?
Tarrant County holds them at the base of the courthouse steps on the west side of the Tarrant County Courthouse, 100 West Weatherford Street in downtown Fort Worth. Sales are on the first Tuesday of every month, and each notice lists its time. If your house has a notice, reach out right away.
Do you buy Fort Worth houses with foundation problems?
Yes, we look at houses with foundation issues, roof damage and other big repairs. We buy as-is, so the condition is part of our offer, not something you fix first.
Can I sell a rental property with tenants in Fort Worth?
In many cases, yes. You don’t necessarily need to wait for a lease to end or turn the unit over. Tell us about the lease and the tenants, and we’ll figure out the best way to handle it.
Do you charge commissions to buy my house in Fort Worth?
No. We’re the buyer, not an agent, so there’s no commission to pay. We also cover typical closing costs.
