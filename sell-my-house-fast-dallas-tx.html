Dallas specifics

Dallas is the seat of Dallas County, so most of the county offices you might deal with during a tough sale are downtown, several of them in one building.

  • Foreclosure sales: The Dallas County Clerk lists the sale location as the north side of the George Allen Courts Building, facing 600 Commerce Street. Sales are held the first Tuesday of every month, and each notice shows its own start time.
  • Probate: Dallas County has three probate courts, Probate Courts No. 1, 2 and 3, on the 7th floor of that same George Allen building.
  • Property taxes: Bills and payments go through the Dallas County Tax Office, led by the county Tax Assessor/Collector. Taxes left unpaid after January 31 start picking up penalty and interest.
  • Code enforcement: The City of Dallas Code Compliance department runs Neighborhood Code and Nuisance Abatement divisions.

If one of these offices has sent you a notice, include it when you reach out. It helps us give you a quicker, more useful answer.

Questions sellers ask

How quickly can I sell my house in Dallas for cash?
Some sellers want to close in a few weeks, and others need more time. Since we aren’t waiting on a mortgage approval, the main factor is clearing the title. Give us your target date and we’ll tell you whether it’s realistic.
Where are foreclosure sales held in Dallas County?
The Dallas County Clerk lists the north side of the George Allen Courts Building, facing 600 Commerce Street, as the sale location. Sales are on the first Tuesday of each month, and each notice gives its start time. If you’ve received a notice, send us the date so we can see whether there’s time to close.
Will you buy my Dallas house if it has code violations?
Yes, we buy houses with open city code cases. You don’t have to make the repairs before you sell. Just tell us what the notice says.
Can I sell an inherited house in Dallas before probate is finished?
Sometimes, depending on who has the authority to sign. Dallas County’s three probate courts handle estates, and the court usually has to grant that authority before a sale can close. We can review the situation with your attorney and the title company.
Do ’we buy houses’ companies in Dallas pay a fair price?
Every offer depends on the house, its condition and what it would take to resell it. We explain how we got to our number and what you’d net after any payoff, so you can compare it with listing. If listing makes more sense for you, we’ll tell you.
